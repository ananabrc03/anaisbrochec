# Privacy policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

## Data controller
Anaïs Brochec, 47 rue Vivienne, 75002 Paris, France · anaisbrochec@yahoo.fr

## Contact form
**Data collected**: last name, first name, email address, company (optional), subject of the request, message and, where applicable, a PDF attachment.
**Purpose**: answering your request (job or internship offer, assignment, web project).
**Legal basis**: your consent, given through the form checkbox.
**Recipient**: Anaïs Brochec only. Data is never sold or shared with third parties.
**Retention**: up to 24 months after the last exchange, then deleted.

## Website analytics
The site measures traffic without cookies and without storing your IP address: page viewed, language, referring site (e.g. linkedin.com), device type and a random identifier valid only for the browser tab, which cannot identify you. CV downloads are counted the same way. This data is kept for 13 months.

## Processors
- **Supabase** (database and attachment storage, servers in the European Union)
- **Vercel** (website hosting)
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** (anti-spam protection for the form)
- **Resend** (notification email to the publisher, if enabled)

## Your rights
Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email anaisbrochec@yahoo.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.

## Cookies
This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Only local technical storage remembers your language choice.
