# Crisis management

## Header

- **Title**: Consultant · crisis management & influencer strategy
- **Period**: August 2026, strategy delivered in 24 hours
- **Short summary**: A well-known business content creator sees his credibility attacked after a controversial video, a few weeks before the event he runs on his own. In 24 hours, I delivered an analysis of public opinion, a recommended stance and an influencer strategy to fill the room. The client applied it the very next day; one month later, the event was sold out.
- **Key qualities**: `Q-REA` Responsiveness & composure · `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills
- **Key skill families**: `MKT` Digital marketing & influence · `ANA` Performance analysis & management

## My role

- Analysis of TikTok videos and comments over three weeks (1 to 20 August 2026)
- Identification of reaction patterns to ground the strategy
- Recommended stance to move past the controversy
- Influencer strategy: 15 micro-influencers identified, a top 5 selected
- Tracking set-up through promo codes
- Seven-week roadmap up to event day

## Context

The client, a well-known business content creator, organises an industry forum in Paris. Unlike previous editions, which brought together several speakers, he will carry this one almost alone.

In mid-August, one of his videos sparks a controversy that spirals and takes over his feed and daily lives. Yet an event carried by one person rests entirely on that person's credibility: precisely what the controversy attacks. Two workstreams follow, with a single goal: **protect the client's credibility, then turn it into attendees**.

## Missions

### 1. Analysing public opinion
- **Sub-skills**: `ANA-4` Social listening & opinion analysis · `ANA-6` Data visualisation
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills · `Q-REA` Responsiveness & composure

I analysed the client's videos, those of other creators and their comments in depth to gauge opinion and identify reaction patterns.

- **The substance was defensible, the tone lit the fire**: a message presented as a universal, guilt-inducing truth. Nothing illegal or morally serious: a controversy about stance.
- **In two weeks, the debate changed nature**: 70% of reactions first targeted the tone and the message; a few days later, more than 60% targeted the client's legitimacy. The tipping point came the day after his first defensive videos.
- **A loyal base clearly present**: the share of support grew from 15% to 30%.
- **A bubble mechanism**: algorithms group content by topic and keep a controversy alive as long as it is fed. Each defensive live restarted the bubble, and a second bubble, about legitimacy, had formed at the worst possible time.

### 2. Recommending a stance
- **Sub-skills**: `MKT-6` Crisis management & online reputation · `STR-6` Strategic diagnosis & recommendations
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills

The client's shift in direction was not wrong in itself: everything depended on the angle. I recommended moving from **"proving"** ("I'll prove I'm not lying", which puts him in the dock and feeds the bubble) to **"teaching"** ("here is what I did, here is how you can do it").
1. Change the angle: from defence to sharing, with a single educational clarification
2. Share other journeys: third-party success stories that calm and inspire
3. Take back control of lives: themed, structured lives on topics useful to the event

Why it works: the data already showed it. Videos with a guest or an international topic lowered the pressure without reigniting the controversy.

### 3. Influencer strategy to fill the event
- **Sub-skills**: `MKT-2` Influencer & affiliate marketing · `ANA-5` Comparative analysis & decision support · `STR-1` Launch strategy & go-to-market
- **Qualities**: `Q-CRE` Creativity · `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills

The idea: turn the bubble mechanism to our advantage and deliberately create a positive bubble around the event, with several voices speaking at the same time. To be deployed only once recovery was under way.
- **15 micro-influencers** identified, then a **top 5** selected on three criteria in this order: engagement, content relevance, audience size
- **Two circles**: for all 15, an invitation, a personal promo code, a press pack and an interview at the stand; for the top 5, in addition, a round table with the client, hotel and travel covered, a networking dinner and a signed invitation
- **Tracking**: each promo code attributes registrations to an influencer, measuring each one's real contribution
- **A win-win exchange** rather than a paid service: visibility, network and prestige for the influencer; qualified audience and attendees for the event

### 4. Roadmap to event day
- **Sub-skills**: `PRO-5` End-to-end project management
- **Qualities**: `Q-REA` Responsiveness & composure

Seven weeks, from 20 August to event day: first put out the fire, then fill the room.
1. **Crisis recovery**: stop defensive lives, one single educational clarification, back to valuable content
2. **Calm & preparation**: content pivot towards the event, influencer shortlist, premium offers, promo codes, press pack
3. **Influencer campaign**: influencer activation, teasing ramp-up, round table and dinner logistics
4. **Final sprint**: ticketing reminders and testimonials, influencer logistics, stand interviews, D-1 briefing
