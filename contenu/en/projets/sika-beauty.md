# Sika Beauty

## Header

- **Title**: Consultant · art direction & social media relaunch
- **Period**: September 2026 · ongoing
- **Short summary**: Sika Beauty, a make-up and nail artist, asked me to relaunch her social media and launch her first press-on nail collection. I structured the collection into eight worlds and defined a "cocktails" art direction that gives each design its place without diluting the message.
- **Key qualities**: `Q-CRE` Creativity · `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills
- **Key skill families**: `DES` Design & art direction · `MKT` Digital marketing & influence · `STR` Strategy & business development

## My role

- Diagnosis of the collection and the existing brand image
- Structuring 25 designs into 8 sub-collections
- Art direction of the launch campaign
- A consistent, repeatable visual identity
- Social media relaunch

## Context

Sika Beauty offers make-up and nail services. Her first press-on nail collection, **"Impériale"**, includes 25 designs. The client wanted to showcase them all, which was impossible without diluting the message.

Another issue: inconsistent visuals, which weakened brand recognition. The target positioning is mid-range, for clients who expect quality services and products.

## Missions

### 1. Structuring the collection
- **Sub-skills**: `STR-6` Strategic diagnosis & recommendations · `DES-2` Collection & campaign art direction · `STR-1` Launch strategy & go-to-market
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills · `Q-CRE` Creativity

Rather than presenting 25 designs on the same level, I started from the designs and the collection name, "Impériale", to form **8 sub-groups by style**. Each has a name and a signature set: eight different ways to make an entrance, eight moods. The message stays clear and every design finds its place.


### 2. "Cocktails" art direction
- **Sub-skills**: `DES-2` Collection & campaign art direction · `DES-1` Brand identity & visual guidelines
- **Qualities**: `Q-CRE` Creativity

Each sub-collection is paired with a cocktail. The choice draws on the calendar (back-to-school season and the return of evening events), on the clientele and on the mid-range positioning: cocktails evoke elegance, occasion and pleasure without tipping into unattainable luxury. Visuals move from identical packshots on a black background to lifestyle compositions that tell a mood.

### 3. Visual identity & social media relaunch
- **Sub-skills**: `DES-1` Brand identity & visual guidelines · `MKT-1` Multichannel social media strategy
- **Qualities**: `Q-RIG` Rigour & high standards

I laid the foundations of a stable visual identity (composition rules, moods, consistency across posts) so the brand gains in consistency and recognition. This identity underpins the relaunch of her social media around the collection launch.
