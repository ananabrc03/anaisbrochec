## Context

Lissolo is an educational and cultural board game about the Republic of the Congo: **1,200 questions, 350 cards, 7 themes**. Its gameplay sits between Snakes and Ladders and Trivial Pursuit.

I joined the project, led by Kriss Brochec, in May 2024, as the first edition was being finalised. My role covered everything between a prototype and a product on shelves: securing quality before production, giving the game an online presence, selling and distributing it in France and in the Congo, then preparing its fundraising.

### 2. Optimising the proofreading process
- **Sub-skills**: `PRO-4` Quality control · `WEB-5` Applied AI & process optimisation · `PRO-6` Team management & training · `PRO-5` End-to-end project management
- **Qualities**: `Q-RIG` Rigour & high standards · `Q-REA` Responsiveness & composure · `Q-LEA` Leadership & ownership

**The problem.** When the first sample arrived, many typos remained despite several proofreads. Writers proofread their own questions, then two dedicated proofreaders went through each block in one go. With short, sometimes technical questions and cards that look alike, proofreading became repetitive: eye strain, skipped questions, missed typos.

**What I put in place.**
- **Sprints**: proofreading in batches of 50 cards, one theme at a time
- **Two teams**: a lighter workload per person and more ownership. Team A flags typos but above all validates the content
- **Local AI model**: trained on the recurring patterns of how questions are written, it spots inconsistencies better and corrects the form. For each batch of 50 questions, it produces statistics on question formats, typo corrections and a summary of the most frequent errors
- **Final review**: team B, which did not do the first read, validates the work of team A and the AI

**Result**: in 5 days, errors were fixed and mass production could start.


### 3. Visual identity & showcase website
- **Sub-skills**: `DES-3` Graphic & print design · `WEB-2` Website creation & front-end development · `DES-4` UX/UI design & wireframing · `WEB-1` E-commerce & sales platforms
- **Qualities**: `Q-ADA` Adaptability & self-directed learning · `Q-CRE` Creativity

I contributed to the project's visual materials: packaging, business cards, roll-up banners and flyers.

Above all, I designed the website, **my first website**, on Hostinger. With no coding knowledge at the time, I pushed the drag & drop builder as far as it would go and added custom code for some features: the accordion FAQ and the mini quizzes for each theme. The main challenge: a polished mobile interface, fine-tuned block by block. When we ran into issues with Stripe, I set up a SumUp e-shop as a fallback for online sales.

**Looking back**, I would improve three things today: a real sales page with integrated payment for conversion, lighter pages for loading speed, and a structure built for search engines. That is exactly what I applied later on [[afro-buffet]].

### 4. Sales in France & the Congo
- **Sub-skills**: `STR-1` Launch strategy & go-to-market · `STR-2` Distribution strategy & negotiation · `MKT-4` Community management & engagement
- **Qualities**: `Q-INT` International & intercultural openness · `Q-ENT` Entrepreneurial mindset

**In France**, I handled B2C sales and represented the game at monthly events (Christmas parties, community events) to raise awareness and build the brand image.

**In the Congo**, I negotiated distribution with two leading retailers: **Fnac in Brazzaville** and **Librairie Paillet in Pointe-Noire**, a true local institution.

### 5. Investment pitch · Innovation Outre-Mer
- **Sub-skills**: `STR-5` Business plan & investor pitch · `ANA-1` Financial analysis & unit costs
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills

For the Innovation Outre-Mer (IOM) forum, where the Congo was the guest of honour, I structured the investment pitch: the building blocks of the deck and its appendices (vision, sales forecasts, cash needs, project scope).

The deck (October 2025 version) covers the full story: key market figures and financial targets, competitive positioning, approach and product lines (national, regional and thematic editions), the Lissolo 2.0 product, early traction and the 2025 · 2027+ roadmap.
