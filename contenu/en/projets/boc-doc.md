# Boc d'Oc

## Header

- **Title**: Strategy & performance analyst (internship)
- **Period**: April to June 2024, 8 weeks
- **Short summary**: Hired for sales strategy, I identified a more urgent need: understanding the company's numbers. I built a financial dashboard still used every month by the management committee, then proposed a strategy to grow direct sales.
- **Key qualities**: `Q-ADA` Adaptability & self-directed learning · `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills · `Q-RIG` Rigour & high standards
- **Key skill families**: `ANA` Performance analysis & management · `STR` Strategy & business development

## My role

- Diagnosed the company's real need, beyond the initial assignment
- Reworked and structured invoices and sales from October 2023 to March 2024
- Designed a monthly Excel management dashboard
- Analysed revenue, sales mix and unit costs
- Reporting slides for the management team
- Benchmarked five competitors and proposed meal plans for direct sales

## Context

Boc d'Oc is a young family business in Montpellier that prepares ready meals in glass jars for individuals and companies, with 95% of ingredients sourced within 50 km. At the time, it was also launching a catering business and looking for its balance.

Hired as a strategy intern with a mostly commercial brief, I quickly saw that the priority lay elsewhere. Invoicing and sales data existed, but the company had no tool to read them: it was the first time it looked at its accounts in a structured way. I offered to take this on, and the assignment was refocused.

## Missions

### 1. Building the management tool
- **Sub-skills**: `ANA-2` Dashboards & management tools · `ANA-1` Financial analysis & unit costs · `ANA-6` Data visualisation · `STR-6` Strategic diagnosis & recommendations
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills · `Q-RIG` Rigour & high standards · `Q-ADA` Adaptability & self-directed learning

I built the tool in four steps:
1. **Collect**: invoices and sales from October 2023 to March 2024
2. **Structure**: five sales segments, six cost categories
3. **Visualise**: revenue, costs, variance to forecast, unit cost of a jar
4. **Share**: reporting slides for management

The analysis revealed a shifting growth engine: sales to professionals drove most of the revenue, then catering and subcontracting took over, before private label took off. In one quarter, private label went from about a fifth to half of revenue. Management can now see where value is created and prioritise its sales efforts.

### 2. Making costs readable for the whole team
- **Sub-skills**: `ANA-1` Financial analysis & unit costs · `ANA-3` KPI tracking & reporting
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills

I broke down the price of a jar (raw materials, labour, container, other costs, margin) and turned the figures into meaningful orders of magnitude for the whole team. For example, covering one team member's salary takes **1,008 jars sold per month**, or 252 per week. The analysis also shows that in-store discounts eat directly into the margin: the same jar brings in €8 excl. VAT in direct sales vs €6.62 in stores.

### 3. Direct-sales strategy
- **Sub-skills**: `STR-4` Market watch, benchmarking & market research · `STR-3` Pricing & offer design · `STR-6` Strategic diagnosis & recommendations
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills · `Q-CRE` Creativity

**The problem**: direct sales were flat, even though it is the most profitable channel per jar. Without meal plans, nothing encouraged customers to order several jars or come back.

**My approach**:
- **Benchmark** of five competitors, revealing two market logics: the dish (packs of 4 to 24 dishes per week) or the full meal (plans for 5 to 20 people minimum)
- **Proposal** of three plans with one simple rule, "one plan = one meal": Solo (main, €8 per meal), Duo (starter + main or main + dessert, €12), Trio (starter + main + dessert, €16.30), 3 meals minimum, with a "maxi" option
- **Pricing logic**: prices built jar by jar, and the lowest price in the benchmark for a full meal, with no minimum number of guests
- **Recommendations**: a minimum basket of three meals, a framework that reassures and speeds up choice, rotation of formats, then a move towards subscription. Supporting levers: make the plans visible on the website and back sales with Meta campaigns, factoring in the jar deposit
