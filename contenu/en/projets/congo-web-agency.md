# Congo Web Agency

## Header

- **Title**: Social Media Lead
- **Period**: 2022 · 2026, five campaign seasons (every summer, plus a spring campaign in 2025)
- **Short summary**: Since 2022, I have run the social media side of a US multinational's promotional campaigns in the Republic of the Congo every summer, on behalf of a full-service communication agency. I started as Community Manager; today I am responsible for the entire online side of the campaign: strategy, advertising, teams, influence and reporting.
- **Key qualities**: `Q-LEA` Leadership & ownership · `Q-INT` International & intercultural openness · `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills
- **Key skill families**: `MKT` Digital marketing & influence · `ANA` Performance analysis & management · `PRO` Product, project & operations

## My role

- Multichannel social media strategy: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp
- Management of Meta ad campaigns
- Recruiting, training and coaching community managers and TikTok creators
- Coordinating internal teams and external providers (video editors)
- Editorial calendars, briefs and brand reference documents
- Online activations: giveaways, lives, challenges
- Campaigns with local micro-influencers: selection, negotiation, briefs, follow-up
- End-of-campaign reporting and year-over-year recommendations

## Context

Congo Web Agency is a full-service communication agency based in the Republic of the Congo. Every summer, it runs promotional campaigns for one of its clients, a US multinational, to promote the brand, drive sales and maintain its image. A confidentiality clause prevents me from naming the client.

My scope grew with the results, from content planning to defining and monitoring the entire strategy:

- **2022 · Community Manager**: planning and publishing, caption writing, community engagement, first analyses
- **2023 · Social Media Manager**: contribution to strategy, platform-specific content, digital activations, launch of TikTok formats
- **Since 2024 · Social Media Lead**: campaign social media strategy, editorial calendar, advertising, coordination of creators and editors, reporting and recommendations
- **2026 · new channel**: WhatsApp community, activating the network of loyal customers


## Missions

### 1. Multichannel social media strategy
- **Sub-skills**: `MKT-1` Multichannel social media strategy · `MKT-5` Social ads (Meta)
- **Qualities**: `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills · `Q-INT` International & intercultural openness

Each network plays a distinct role in the campaign:
- **Facebook**: maximise visibility (ads, lives, giveaways)
- **Instagram**: build the relationship (recipe Reels, interactions)
- **TikTok**: frequent, spontaneous formats (daily posts, local creators)
- **WhatsApp** (2026): build loyalty through a channel for loyal customers

Targeted advertising drives reach: in 2024, 95% of the people reached on Facebook were reached through ads. In 2025, sharper audience targeting and more sponsored videos more than doubled reach.

### 3. Team management & coordination
- **Sub-skills**: `PRO-6` Team management & training · `PRO-4` Quality control

I select the right profiles (content creation, editing), train and coach the TikTok creator team and the community managers. I coordinate all internal and external parties, including video editors, to ensure regular posts that meet the client's standards. I review deliverables before publication.
