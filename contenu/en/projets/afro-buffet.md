# Afro Buffet

## Header

- **Title**: Founder · Product, operations & go-to-market
- **Period**: November 2025 · present
- **Short summary**: Creating and launching Afro Buffet, a board game brand dedicated to African cuisine. I lead the first game from concept to mass production, along with its go-to-market strategy, its digital ecosystem and the development of the range.
- **Key qualities**: `Q-ENT` Entrepreneurial mindset · `Q-ADA` Adaptability & self-directed learning · `Q-RIG` Rigour & high standards
- **Key skill families**: `PRO` Product, project & operations · `STR` Strategy & business development · `WEB` Web, AI & automation

## My role

I run the project end to end:
- Game concept and game design (7 game modes, 42 dishes)
- Desk research and content writing
- Brand identity, cards, packaging and print materials
- Industrial sourcing: 20 suppliers identified, 10 consulted, 1 selected
- Production follow-up and European compliance (EN71, CE)
- Pricing, launch and distribution strategy
- Custom-coded Shopify store and recipe website
- Content creation and an AI-accelerated video process

## Context

Afro Buffet is a range of games about African food and cultures. Its first product, **Afro Buffet Original**, is a 7-in-1 card game featuring 42 dishes from across the continent. Three more games are planned in the range, including **Saka Saka**, created by Kriss Brochec.

The project grew out of my experience at [[lissolo-games]] and one observation: there is demand for games that represent African cultures, but existing initiatives remain isolated. They often stop at a single game, with no range strategy and no structured distribution network.

The ambition is to build a genuine publishing house. The African market combines a young, growing population, rising purchasing power and a need for cultural roots; games are also a way to get children away from screens and to tell African stories beyond books. Food is a universal entry point: a topic that speaks to everyone and carries a lot of emotion. The real challenge then lies in distribution, in Europe and across the continent.

## Missions

### 1. Product design
- **Sub-skills**: `PRO-1` Product design · `DES-3` Graphic & print design · `DES-1` Brand identity & visual guidelines
- **Qualities**: `Q-CRE` Creativity · `Q-RIG` Rigour & high standards

I designed the concept of Afro Buffet Original, its seven game mechanics and the structure of its content, then carried out the desk research needed to select and present the 42 dishes in the game.

I created the cards on Canva and the packaging on Illustrator, adapting colours to print constraints to get a faithful result. This phase meant constantly connecting brand vision, player experience and the technical realities of manufacturing.

### 2. Sourcing & production
- **Sub-skills**: `PRO-2` Sourcing & supplier negotiation · `PRO-3` Industrialisation & compliance · `ANA-5` Comparative analysis & decision support · `PRO-5` End-to-end project management
- **Qualities**: `Q-RIG` Rigour & high standards · `Q-INT` International & intercultural openness · `Q-ANL` Analytical & synthesis skills

I built and ran the industrial sourcing process:
- **Identification**: 5 suppliers met at the Cannes International Games Festival, 15 sourced on Alibaba
- **Shortlist**: 10 companies meeting our criteria, contacted directly
- **Specifications**: a Product Requirements Document (PRD) sent to all 10 suppliers
- **Quotes**: EXW and DDP prices and shipping options compared for 500, 1,000 and 2,000 units
- **Selection**: trade-off between supplier expertise, ease of communication and price. Suppliers not selected remain in our database for future projects
- **Follow-up**: managed all exchanges with the chosen supplier, from sample to delivery of the mass production run
- **Compliance**: negotiated EN71 testing and the documents required for CE marking to sell in Europe

First production run: **1,000 units**.


### 3. Launch & distribution strategy
- **Sub-skills**: `STR-1` Launch strategy & go-to-market · `STR-2` Distribution strategy & negotiation · `STR-3` Pricing & offer design · `STR-4` Market watch, benchmarking & market research · `MKT-2` Influencer & affiliate marketing · `WEB-1` E-commerce & sales platforms
- **Qualities**: `Q-ENT` Entrepreneurial mindset · `Q-INT` International & intercultural openness

I set the pricing, the B2C launch plan and a step-by-step distribution strategy: test the market directly, build proof of traction, then approach larger retailers.

- **TikTok Shop**, the number-one B2C launch channel: application to the restricted "Toys & games" category, product page, selection of affiliate content creators
- **First retail listing**: Le Temple du Jeu
- **Central and West Africa**: distribution plan in progress, aiming for mass-retail presence by summer 2027

Partnerships with large retailers remain goals, not signed agreements at this stage.

### 4. Digital ecosystem
- **Sub-skills**: `WEB-1` E-commerce & sales platforms · `WEB-2` Website creation & front-end development · `WEB-4` AI-assisted development · `DES-4` UX/UI design & wireframing · `DES-1` Brand identity & visual guidelines
- **Qualities**: `Q-ADA` Adaptability & self-directed learning · `Q-CRE` Creativity

I designed two complementary websites sharing one identity.

**"Afro Fiesta" Shopify store**. After the wireframes, site architecture and visual guidelines, I built a fully custom Shopify theme in Liquid with the help of Claude Code. The goal: avoid a standard theme, extend the game's visual world and make the product understandable in seconds to convert better (rules in 3 steps, carousel of the 7 game modes, bundled launch offer, quick facts on players, duration, modes and cards).

**Recipe website**. I coded a companion site in HTML, CSS and JavaScript gathering the game's 42 recipes, deployed on Vercel. One recipe template duplicated for each dish; an index organised into category menus using the cards' colours; a recipe recommendation system at the bottom of each page.

### 5. Content creation & video process
- **Sub-skills**: `MKT-3` Content creation & video production · `WEB-5` Applied AI & process optimisation · `DES-5` AI-assisted visual creation
- **Qualities**: `Q-ADA` Adaptability & self-directed learning · `Q-CRE` Creativity

I design and shoot the brand's launch content. To keep up the publishing pace, I set up an AI-accelerated post-production process:
- **Descript** to prepare and sort footage
- **ChatCut** to speed up the first edit
- **Higgsfield** for some motion design elements
- **DaVinci Resolve** to manually refine rhythm, transitions, sound, colour grading and finishing

AI handles the repetitive tasks; creative direction and control over the final result stay human. The goal is not just to produce faster, but to have a repeatable content system that supports sales over time.
